Principal Investigator

Jeonghan Yu, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering, SUNY Korea

Her research integrates robotic mechanism design, intelligent control, and human biomechanics to develop robotic systems that understand and assist human movement. Her work spans wearable and rehabilitation robotics, autonomous mechanism synthesis, and quantitative movement assessment, with the goal of enabling adaptive and personalized robotic assistance.

Academic Appointments & Research Experience

  • Since 2026: Assistant Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering, State University of New York, Korea (SUNY Korea, Stony Brook University)
  • 2024–2026: Postdoctoral Research Scientist, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Columbia University, United States @ Robotics and Rehabilitation Lab
  • 2024: Postdoctoral Researcher, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Chung-Ang University, South Korea @ Assistive and Rehabilitation Robotics Lab
  • 2017–2024: Doctoral Researcher, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Seoul National University, South Korea @ Integrated Design and Analysis of Myltiphysics Systems Lab
  • 2013–2017: Undergraduate Student (Research Intern), Department of Mechanical Engineeirng, Seoul National University, South Korea
