Who we are

About the Lab

HEARD Lab stands for Human-centered Engineering and Autonomous Robot Design. We are a research group in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at SUNY Korea.

Our mission is to create robotic systems that are grounded in the mechanics of both machines and people. We combine robotic hardware, control, biomechanical sensing, and computational design to understand movement and develop personalized assistance.

We see design, control, and movement analysis as complementary parts of the same engineering problem: creating robots that work with people.

What we do

Research Directions

From new mechanisms to meaningful human assistance — three connected research themes.

01 / ROBOTICS

Wearable & Rehabilitation Robotics

Reconfigurable cable-driven robots, exoskeleton mechanisms, and human-in-the-loop assistance for functional movement.

02 / HUMAN MOVEMENT

Movement & Biomechanics

Whole-body motion analysis, postural stability, coordination, and quantitative metrics for movement assessment.

03 / DESIGN

Autonomous Robot Design

Mechanism synthesis, topology optimization, and computational methods that transform functional needs into robotic designs.
